THE RULES

Three things to know.

  1. Step 01 · The rate1 Stall = 25,000 $AMKT, counted from the Stalls your wallet holds at the moment you claim. Presale purchases claim separately, at the full bought amount.
  2. Step 02 · Claim opensClaims open the moment the presale ends. The presale runs 14 days from its start, and the countdown above follows the on-chain end time.
  3. Step 03 · ClaimYour Binance Agentic Wallet agent calls the claim functions and the $AMKT lands in the same wallet. Each of the two claims can be used once per wallet, and neither carries a fee.

Agent prompt · claim

You are my Binance Agentic Wallet agent. Claim my $AMKT on Agent Market (BNB Chain, chain ID 56).

Claim contract: 0xCE87A2D9b19b53197167136921AC16423CefDf8e
Stall contract: 0x24C9191B529aC2aBAa19af4b11eBB1fdeB74D575

Steps:
1. Read endTime() (selector 0x3197cbb6). If the presale has not ended yet, tell me when claims open and stop.
2. Read claimable(my wallet) (selector 0x402914f5), bought(my wallet) (selector 0x667022fd), and my Stall balance (balanceOf, selector 0x70a08231, on the Stall contract).
3. If I bought in the presale and presaleClaimed(my wallet) (selector 0xf9765bc1) is false, call claimPresale() (selector 0x046ef9a5).
4. If I hold Stalls and holderClaimed(my wallet) (selector 0xdd6f5c53) is false, call claimHolder() (selector 0x6f19bc8b). It pays 25,000 AMKT per Stall.
5. Confirm my $AMKT balance afterwards and report what I received. Both claims are free, with no fee.

Worked example: 10 Stalls = 250,000 AMKT. The most one wallet can hold is 25 Stalls, worth 625,000 AMKT, plus whatever it bought in the presale.
