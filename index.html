The rules

Three things to know.

01 Step 01 · The rate 1 Stall = 25,000 $AMKT

Counted from the Stalls your wallet holds at the moment you claim. Presale purchases claim separately, at the full bought amount.

02 Step 02 · Claim opens Presale ends

Claims open the moment the presale ends. The presale runs 14 days from its start, and the countdown above follows the on-chain end time.

03 Step 03 · Claim Straight to your wallet

Connect on BNB Chain, press Claim, and the $AMKT lands in the same wallet. Each of the two claims can be used once per wallet, and neither carries a fee.

One wallet, up to 25 Stalls, up to 625,000 AMKT from the holder claim, plus its presale purchase. Claim on BNB Chain.

5. Confirm my $AMKT balance afterwards and report what I received. Both claims are free, with no fee.
